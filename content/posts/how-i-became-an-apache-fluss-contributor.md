I started exploring open source seriously with the goal of improving my backend and distributed systems knowledge.

That journey led me to Apache Fluss, where I began contributing through documentation, testing, and client-related improvements. Over time, I became more comfortable understanding the project structure, reading existing code, and participating in technical discussions.

In this blog, I will document my journey, what I learn from open source, and the work I do around distributed systems, Apache Fluss, and GSoC.
